**Why CC BY rather than MIT:** MIT is a software licence, and most of what is
here is writing. The point of these documents is that people quote them, argue
with them, translate them, and paste them into their own wikis and forum posts.
CC BY says yes to all of that and asks only that the trail leads back here — so
that when something turns out to be wrong, the correction can find its way to
the same readers.
